# BNBU Sports Privacy Policy

Version: 2.3

Updated: 24 August 2026

Effective: 24 August 2026

BNBU Sports is operated for sports teaching administration at Beijing Normal University at Zhuhai. This policy applies to the student app, teacher services, and related course, attendance, activity-hour and grade services. It explains how we handle personal information and how you can exercise your rights.

## 1. General terms

We process information only for clear, necessary and transparent purposes, with appropriate security measures. We do not deny unrelated basic functions because you decline optional processing. Please read this policy before registering, signing in or using the service.

## 2. Information we process

To provide account and teaching services, we may process your student ID, name, school affiliation, class, email address, account credentials, course relationship, attendance, activity records, grades, application materials and review results. Students sign in with a one-time email code. Teachers and administrators use an email address and password. If a student cannot access email, a school administrator must verify the student's identity before adding or correcting the email address. We process photos, videos and files only when you choose a feature that needs them, such as an activity check-in, exemption application, appeal or feedback.

## 3. Android permissions

CAMERA is used after your action to scan a course invitation QR code or capture on-site check-in evidence. RECORD_AUDIO is requested only together with CAMERA when you choose in-app video recording, because check-in video must contain sound. Active recording is limited to 15 seconds, paused time is excluded, and the result is compressed on-device before upload. Denying CAMERA prevents on-site photos and video; denying only RECORD_AUDIO prevents video but does not affect on-site photos or manual invitation-code entry. The app does not request precise, approximate or background location, broad media-storage, contacts, SMS, call logs or installed-app-list permissions. Photo and video selection uses the system picker for files you choose.

## 4. Course, check-in and application processing

Course invitations, enrolment requests, check-ins, exercise duration, supporting materials, exemption applications and grades are processed to deliver the teaching workflow, prevent duplicate submissions and allow authorised teachers to review records. Media drafts remain on the device until submitted or discarded; submitted materials are retained with the related teaching record under applicable school retention rules.

## 5. Device information

The current app does not request location permission, obtain coordinates or create travel tracks for check-in evidence. Technical requests may include device model, Android/app version, language, network state, time zone, IP address, request path and status code for security and troubleshooting.

## 6. Notifications and service providers

Firebase Cloud Messaging may use an installation identifier and push token to deliver course and service notifications. Tokens are associated with your account only for notifications and are not used for advertising. Service providers that process information on our behalf must follow applicable privacy and security obligations.

## 7. Use, sharing and disclosure

We use personal information only to provide, secure, maintain and improve the teaching service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. We share information only with authorised school staff, processors or recipients where necessary and lawful, and disclose it publicly only when required by law or with a lawful basis.

## 8. Storage and security

Information is stored for the period needed for the relevant teaching, account, audit or legal purpose. We apply access control, encryption in transit, secure storage, logging and incident-response measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely risk-free; if an incident requires notice, we will act as required by law.

## 9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, explanation, withdrawal of consent or account-related assistance. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already completed on a valid basis, and may prevent functions that depend on that information. Contact the school sports teaching administration through the official campus support channel for requests.

## 10. Children and policy updates

The service is primarily for university students, teachers and administrators. Users under 18 should read this policy with a guardian; processing for children under 14 requires guardian authorisation where applicable. We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change and will provide notice for material changes.

## 11. Contact

For questions, requests or complaints about this policy or personal information, contact the Beijing Normal University at Zhuhai sports teaching administration through the official campus support channel. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and school procedures.

## 12. Important review notice

This English version is provided for use in the English app interface. It requires formal legal review by the institution before external publication; the Chinese version remains the reference text pending that review.
